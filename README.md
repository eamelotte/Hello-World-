## Project Title 
Study Iowa City- Best Places to Study in Iowa City 

## Description 
This repository serves as a guide to identify the best study locations across Iowa City! Whether you need a quiet zone or a busy coffee shop, or an late night campus study spot- this project will give you all the information that you need. The goal of this project is to help students find reliable study spots that they can use for their years spent at The University of Iowa 

## Tools Used 
For this project, I used markdown, python and SQL, as well as Excel and also a variety of different websites all about study spots in Iowa City
